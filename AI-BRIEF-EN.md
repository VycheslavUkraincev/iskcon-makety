## 1. ABOUT THE PROJECT

**What it is:** The official website of the religious community of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) in Chernivtsi, Ukraine.

**Domain:** https://iskcon.cv.ua/
**Preview:** https://vycheslavukraincev.github.io/iskcon-cv-ua/
**Language:** Ukrainian (primary). Additionally: English, Română.

**Site goal:** Bring a person off the street to the Sunday program. One practical step: "Come on Sunday at 13:30".

### 7 site objectives (from the Figma presentation):
1. Introduce the movement — what ISKCON is
2. Show the path to joining — a newcomer's first steps
3. Reveal the value of practice — meditation, Vedic lifestyle
4. Dispel doubts — answer stereotypes ("is it a cult?")
5. Engage in service — volunteers for "Food for Life"
6. Access to knowledge — books, lectures
7. Live communication — connection with the community

## 6. ANSWERS TO HARD QUESTIONS

**"Is it a cult?"**
No. ISKCON is an officially registered religious organization, operating in Ukraine since the 1990s. We do not isolate people, do not take property, do not forbid communication with family.

**"Will they force me to buy something?"**
No. Entry is free, the meal is free. Books — only if you wish.

**"Do I have to radically change my life?"**
No. Try the mantra, listen to a lecture — then decide for yourself.
